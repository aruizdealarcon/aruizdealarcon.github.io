Alberto Ruiz-de-Alarcón

I am a mathematician working on quantum many-body systems and quantum information.

Currently, I am an Assistant Professor on the Tenure Track at CUNEF Universidad in Madrid, Spain.

You can contact me at , and find me at Google Scholar, ORCID, and arXiv.

Research interests

My current interests include analytical results on:

  • Emergent phenomena in quantum spin systems, topological phases and anyons.
  • Tensor network states, their symmetries and their renormalization.
  • Open quantum systems, mixed states and dissipative quantum dynamics.
  • Theory of (weak) Hopf algebras, quantum algebra and operator algebras.
